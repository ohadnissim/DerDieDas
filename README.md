# Der Die Das

A der/die/das practice game for young children on iPad. No reading needed: every word is a picture, and the iPad says it in German.

- Tap the play button. The app says the word with its article ("der Hund"), then the child taps **der** (blue ●), **die** (red ▲) or **das** (green ■).
- The child says the word out loud with its hand sign (der ✊ fist, die ☝️ pointing finger, das ✋ flat hand), then taps the arrow. A peace sign ✌️ celebrates each right answer, and short sentences ("Der Hund bellt.") put the word in context.
- Missed words come back a few cards later. Across days, known words come back less often.
- Grown-ups: tap ⚙️ and answer the sum to edit the word list, paste a school list (`der Hund 🐶 | Der Hund bellt.`, one per line) or pick the German voice.
- Pictures in `img/` are clay-style illustrations; words without a picture show their emoji.

Words and progress are saved in the browser on the device (no account, nothing sent anywhere).

**Play it:** https://ohadnissim.github.io/DerDieDas/ (after enabling GitHub Pages: Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch → `main` / root).
